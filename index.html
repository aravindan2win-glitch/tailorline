How the score works. Tailorline pulls the most important terms from the job description, checks how many already appear in your resume, and weights them by how often the posting mentions them, then re-scores after the rewrite. Neither engine invents jobs, dates, or metrics — you stay in control of every word.

Two engines. Free runs entirely in your browser on transparent rules (no key, no cost) — it applies an anti-AI tone pass and adds the skills you approve. AI sends the extracted text plus the JD to Gemini or OpenAI (your key) and returns cleanly structured HTML — far more reliable formatting on messy resumes. Because browsers block external API calls inside this in-app preview and any hosted-artifact sandbox, AI mode only runs when you open the downloaded file in your own browser, or once the tool is hosted. For a public product, the API key belongs on a small server, not shipped to users.
