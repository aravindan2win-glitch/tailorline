How the score works. Tailorline pulls the most important terms from the job description, checks how many already appear in your resume, and weights them by how often the posting mentions them, then re-scores after the rewrite. Neither engine invents jobs, dates, or metrics — you stay in control of every word.

How the rewrite works. Your resume and the job description are sent to a secure server that runs the AI rewrite and returns cleanly formatted, ATS-readable text — your resume is not stored. If the service is momentarily busy, an instant in-browser formatter takes over so you always get a result. Job listings come from public job feeds; "Apply" takes you directly to the employer's own page.
